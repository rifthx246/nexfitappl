LEARN THE MOVEMENTS

Exercise library

A few simple movements to explore. Technique comes before intensity.

01

Bodyweight squat

Practice sitting your hips back and standing with control. Use a comfortable range.

02

Wall push-up

Place your hands on a stable wall and practice a controlled push away.

03

Bird-dog

From hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg only as far as you can stay steady.

04

Glute bridge

Lie on your back with knees bent and lift your hips gently, keeping the movement controlled.

05

Standing calf raise

Hold a stable support if needed and slowly rise onto your toes, then lower.
